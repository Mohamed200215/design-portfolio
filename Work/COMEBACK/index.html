Comeback

SELF-INITIATED // BRAND IDENTITY CONCEPT COMEBACK energy drink brand identity — a full system built around momentum and resilience: bolt-C logomark, electric-blue color language, custom display type, and a four-flavor lineup (Original, Watermelon, Lemon, Mango). Rolled out across packaging, apparel, street posters, and OOH billboards under one rule — flavors change color, the mark never does. No real client — a self-initiated concept built to explore a full brand system end to end. scroll down ↓
THE BRIEF

Energy drinks tend to sell one of two stories: extreme-sports aggression (wings, cliff jumps) or clinical "focus" (nootropic startups). COMEBACK's brief was to own a third, more specific emotional beat — the moment right after you've been knocked down — and build a mark and voice that could physically sit on a shelf next to Red Bull, Monster, and Prime without reading as a fan-made knockoff.

KEY DECISIONS
  • Bolt-C logomark fuses the initial with a lightning strike — "the strike is the split second the comeback begins" — one mark doing double duty as icon and story.
  • Volt Blue against Midnight Navy/Chalk White carries a "spark in the dark" idea through every touchpoint, while each of the four flavors gets its own accent inside the same shell.
  • "IM BACK!!" as a war-cry tagline, not a tidy slogan — built to be shouted on posters and apparel, while the can itself carries the calmer brand statement.
  • Applications lean street/OOH (weathered posters, warehouse signage) over polished lifestyle photography, since the brand's whole premise is "beat up but still standing."
